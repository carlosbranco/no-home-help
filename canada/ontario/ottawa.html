Homeless and housing-crisis help in Ottawa

Immediate danger, serious injury, overdose, fire or a crime in progress: call 911. Shelter placement and City financial assistance: call 311. Food and community-service referrals: call 211.

Last verified: 2026-11-24

Shelters and services can be full, change hours or require an assessment or referral. Call before travelling whenever possible. No listing guarantees a bed, housing, treatment, benefit, payment or job.

If one shelter is full, call 311 and try the other local services below. Say who needs help, your age, whether children or a pet are with you, and any urgent health, safety, accessibility or language needs. Ask about overflow, day programs, outreach and the next safe option rather than relying on one address.

Emergency help and referral

911 — emergency services

Call for immediate danger, serious injury, suspected overdose, fire or a crime in progress. Give the exact address or nearest intersection and explain the immediate risk.

911

ottawa.ca/en/who-should-i-call

24 hours

Call 911. For a non-emergency police matter, Ottawa Police can be reached at 613-236-1222.

City of Ottawa — emergency shelter access

The City coordinates placement into adult, youth and family emergency shelters. Shelter stays may include meals, case management and housing support. Capacity changes, and a call does not guarantee a bed.

311

ottawa.ca/en/residents/social-services/housing/emergency-shelters

311 is available 24 hours

Call 311 and ask for emergency housing. Say whether you are an adult, youth or family and describe accessibility, health and safety needs. Ask for overflow and a day-program alternative if no suitable space is immediately available.
