Homeless and housing-crisis help in Edmonton

Immediate danger, serious injury, overdose, fire or a crime in progress: call 911. For non-emergency crisis outreach and transport to a safe place, call 211 and press 3.

Last verified: 2026-11-24

Shelters and services can be full, change hours or require an assessment or referral. Call before travelling whenever possible. No listing guarantees a bed, housing, treatment, benefits or a job.

If one shelter is full, call 211 and try the other local options below. Say your age, who is with you, whether you have a pet, and any health, safety, accessibility or cultural needs. Ask about outreach, transport, day services and the next safe option rather than relying on one address.
