APIA - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: Police 995. Medical emergency: ambulance 996. Violence or emergency shelter: Samoa Victim Support Group 27904 or 25392.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Samoa Victim Support Group is the main published referral for crisis shelter in Apia. For other housing or welfare needs, contact the Ministry of Women, Community and Social Development; accommodation is not guaranteed and may depend on assessment and family circumstances.
