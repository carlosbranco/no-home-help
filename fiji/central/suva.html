SUVA - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger or medical emergency: 911. Domestic violence helpline: 1560. Child helpline: 1325.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

For a safe place to stay, contact the Salvation Army or a domestic-violence support service before travelling. Suva has separate accommodation programmes for young women and young men; admission depends on the programme's criteria and available places.
