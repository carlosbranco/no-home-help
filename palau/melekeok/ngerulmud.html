NGERULMUD - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger or medical emergency: 911. Violence support: Victims of Crime Advocates 775-8622. Mental-health crisis: 775-8255.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Ngerulmud is a small government centre. The Eastern Community Health Center serves Melekeok, but most social, legal and hospital services are in Koror, roughly 30 kilometres away. No general walk-in homeless shelter is publicly listed. Call services before travelling and confirm transport.
