WELLINGTON - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger or medical emergency: 111. Nowhere to stay: Work and Income 0800 559 009; age 65 or older 0800 552 002.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

If you need somewhere to stay tonight, call Work and Income before paying for accommodation. DCM supports people who are rough sleeping or without shelter, while Whakamaru and the Compassion Soup Kitchen provide practical daytime help, meals, showers and referrals.

Emergency help

Work and Income emergency housing

Government assessment for people who have nowhere to stay tonight or within the next seven days. Work and Income considers other options first, then may arrange transitional or emergency accommodation if the person qualifies and space is available.

Phone service and Wellington service centres

+64800559009

workandincome.govt.nz/housing/nowhere-to-stay/emergency-housing/apply

Phone weekdays 7:00am-6:00pm and Saturday 8:00am-1:00pm

Call before booking or paying for a motel. People aged 65 or older can call 0800 552 002.

Shelter and housing

Emergency and transitional housing assessment

Work and Income is the official route for urgent emergency housing and referrals to funded transitional housing. Emergency housing is short-term and usually reviewed after seven nights.

Wellington service centres and phone

+64800559009

workandincome.govt.nz/housing/nowhere-to-stay

Check phone and service-centre hours

Call first. Same-day accommodation is not guaranteed.
