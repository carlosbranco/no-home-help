Emergency help

Dublin Region Homeless Executive Freephone

Call if you need emergency accommodation. Staff assess your situation and available options; a call does not guarantee a bed. State clearly if you have children or are at immediate risk.

1800 707 707

homelessdublin.ie

Daily; check the current hours on Homeless Dublin

Shelter and housing

Central Placement Service

The central access point for emergency accommodation in Dublin. Bring identification if available, but still ask for help if documents are missing.

Parkgate Hall, 6–9 Conyngham Road, Dublin 8

1800 707 707

dubsimon.ie/what-we-do-1/emergency-outreach/emergency-shelter

Call the Freephone before travelling and ask where to present that day.

Dublin Simon emergency shelter

Emergency beds for single adults and couples are accessed through the Central Placement Service, not guaranteed by arriving directly at the shelter.

dubsimon.ie/what-we-do-1/emergency-outreach/emergency-shelter

Ask the Central Placement Service about Harcourt Street or Carman's Hall and current availability.

Government benefits and social support

Homeless day services

Official local guidance lists day centres for food, showers, laundry, health, phone charging and housing support. Services and opening times vary.

sdcc.ie/en/services/housing/finding-a-home/homeless-services/

Use the current list to choose a suitable centre and call first where possible.

Legal aid

Threshold housing advice

Free advice for renters at risk of losing their home, including notices, rent problems and tenancy rights. It is not an emergency shelter service.

1800 454 454

threshold.ie/get-help/

Monday to Friday; check current telephone hours
