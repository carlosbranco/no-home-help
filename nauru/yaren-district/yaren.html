YAREN - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger or medical emergency: contact Nauru Police or RON Hospital. Legal help: Office of the Public Legal Defender in Yaren.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Nauru does not publish a general homelessness shelter or central intake service. Family and community networks are important, but people who are unsafe should contact police, RON Hospital or the domestic-violence support network. Social Welfare and the Public Legal Defender are both based in Yaren.
