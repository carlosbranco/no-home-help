MBABANE — LUSITO KUBANTFU LABANGENALIKHAYA

Nangabe kunengoti lephutfumako, shayela tinsita tesimo lesiphutfumako letibhalwe ngentasi. Timo tendzawo tingaphazamisa tinsita.

Kugcine kuhlolwa: 2026-11-24

Lwati, tikhatsi, imigomo netindzawo kungashintja. Shayela ungakahambi. Loluhlu alucinisekisi umbhede, kudla, imali, kwelashwa noma umsebenti.

Cala ngensita lefanele ngentasi. Shano kutsi useMbabane, kutsi unayo yini indzawo lephephile kulobusuku, netidzingo temphilo, kuphepha, kukhubateka noma kuvikelwa kwemntfwana.
