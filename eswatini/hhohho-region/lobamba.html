LOBAMBA — LUSITO KUBANTFU LABANGENALIKHAYA

Nangabe kunengoti lephutfumako, shayela tinsita tesimo lesiphutfumako letibhalwe ngentasi. Timo tendzawo tingaphazamisa tinsita.

Kugcine kuhlolwa: 2026-11-24

Lwati, tikhatsi, imigomo netindzawo kungashintja. Shayela ungakahambi. Loluhlu alucinisekisi umbhede, kudla, imali, kwelashwa noma umsebenti.

Cala ngensita lefanele ngentasi. Shano kutsi useLobamba, kutsi unayo yini indzawo lephephile kulobusuku, netidzingo temphilo, kuphepha, kukhubateka noma kuvikelwa kwemntfwana.

Lusito loluphutfumako

National emergency helplines

National emergency helplines iniketa noma ihlele lusito lwe lusito loluphutfumako eLobamba. Tsintsana nabo kucinisekisa imigomo, indzawo nekutsi lusito luyatfolakala.

Eswatini

Police 999; ambulance 977; fire 933; women and children 951; child helpline 116

giyh.gov.sz/helpline

24 hours for emergency lines

Shayela noma ubuke iwebhusayithi lesemtsetfweni ungakahambi. Buta lusito lolukhona namuhla nemibhalo ledzingekako.
