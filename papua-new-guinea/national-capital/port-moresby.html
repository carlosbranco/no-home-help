PORT MORESBY - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: Port Moresby Police 180 0100. Family and sexual violence helpline: 7150 8000. Safe transport for women at risk: 7222 1234.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Port Moresby does not have a single public homelessness intake line. City Mission provides residential programmes for specific groups, while survivors of violence can contact the 1-Tok helpline, Femili PNG or the Meri Seif Line for case management, safe transport and accommodation referrals.

Health care

Port Moresby General Hospital

Main public referral hospital in Port Moresby. The Family Support Centre assists survivors of violence and can provide medical care and social-work referrals.

3 Mile, Port Moresby

pomgen.gov.pg

Emergency department operates continuously

For violence-related care, ask for the Family Support Centre or a social worker. Emergency care comes first.
