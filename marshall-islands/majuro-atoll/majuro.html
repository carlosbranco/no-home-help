MAJURO - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger or medical emergency: 911. Violence, shelter and emergency support for women and girls: WUTMI 625-4296 or 625-5290.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Majuro does not publish a general walk-in homeless shelter. Women United Together Marshall Islands provides emergency casework, shelter and material or financial support for women and girls. Other residents can contact the Marshall Islands Red Cross or government social services, but assistance depends on assessment and current programmes.
