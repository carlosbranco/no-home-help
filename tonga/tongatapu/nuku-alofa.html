NUKU'ALOFA - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: contact Tonga Police. Violence or safe housing: Women and Children Crisis Centre 0800 444. Medical emergency: Vaiola Hospital.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

There is no published general walk-in homeless shelter in Nuku'alofa. Women and children escaping violence can contact WCCC for safe housing. The Salvation Army explicitly states that it does not provide accommodation locally, but it can provide emergency food, clothing, health outreach and other support.
