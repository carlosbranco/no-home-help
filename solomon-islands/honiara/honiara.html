HONIARA - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: Police 999. Violence support and safe referrals: Seif Ples 132; Family Support Centre 26999.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Honiara does not publish a general walk-in homeless shelter. People escaping violence can use the SAFENET referral network, which links police, health, social welfare, legal help, counselling and the Christian Care Centre. Call before travelling because refuge locations and vacancies are controlled for safety.
