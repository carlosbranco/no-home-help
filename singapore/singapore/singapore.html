Singapore — homeless and housing-crisis help

Police: 999. Ambulance or fire: 995. ComCare: 1800 222 0000. For a rough sleeper in immediate danger, call emergency services; otherwise use Help Neighbour or ComCare.

Last verified: 2026-11-24

Services, opening times, eligibility and available places can change. Call first when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, payment, treatment or job.

Say where you are, whether you have a safe place tonight, any urgent illness or injury, whether children are with you, and which identification documents you have.

Emergency help

ComCare Hotline

Ask for the nearest Social Service Office or Family Service Centre, financial help, and a homelessness or shelter assessment. State where you are and whether you need a safe place tonight.

1800 222 0000

Shelter and housing

Safe, Sound Sleeping Places (S3Ps)

Free interim overnight spaces in community premises, with a safe place to sleep, wash and charge a phone. Access is normally through an SSO or FSC assessment; locations and places change.

1800 222 0000

msf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/research-data/report-on-the-street-count-of-rough-sleepers-2025.pdf

Transitional shelters

Temporary shared accommodation for individuals and families who have exhausted other housing options, with onsite social work and a longer-term housing plan. Start at an FSC or SSO; a same-day bed is not guaranteed.

1800 222 0000

Community and religious help

Family Service Centres

Walk-in social work support for housing, family and personal crises, counselling and referrals. Use the MSF directory or ComCare to locate the nearest centre.

1800 222 0000

msf.gov.sg/our-services/directories

Typically weekdays; confirm with the centre

Government benefits and social support

Social Service Offices — ComCare

Financial assistance for low-income people and families, including help with basic living costs. Staff can also connect rough sleepers to employment and shelter services. Bring ID and financial information if available.

1800 222 0000

Employment

Workforce Singapore Career Centres

Career guidance, job matching and training support for Singapore citizens and permanent residents. Ask an SSO or FSC for help if you lack internet, an address or documents.

Other useful resources

Help Neighbour — rough sleeper referral

With the person’s agreement where possible, submit their location and needs so a social service agency can follow up. Do not photograph or share personal details publicly.
