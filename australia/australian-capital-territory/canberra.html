CANBERRA - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger or medical emergency: 000. No safe place to sleep: FIRST Canberra 1800 176 468.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

If you have nowhere safe to sleep, contact FIRST Canberra before going to an accommodation service. It is the ACT central intake service and can assess your situation and connect you with available housing and support. During the day, the Early Morning Centre is a practical place for food, showers, phone access and referrals.

Emergency help

FIRST Canberra

ACT central intake and referral service for people who are homeless or at risk. Staff assess needs, explain housing options and connect people with crisis accommodation and support. A placement depends on assessment and availability.

Canberra and the ACT

+611800176468

act.gov.au/housing-planning-and-property/help/help-if-youre-experiencing-homelessness

Monday to Friday 8:00am-6:00pm; Saturday and Sunday 12:30pm-5:00pm

Call first. Do not travel to a shelter expecting a bed without an assessment.
