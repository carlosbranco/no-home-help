JUBA - HOMELESS HELP

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the local emergency services listed below. Conflict, weather and local conditions can disrupt services.

Last verified: 2026-11-24

Information, hours, eligibility and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Start with the most relevant service below and explain that you are in Juba, whether you have a safe place tonight, and any medical, safety, disability or child-protection needs.

Shelter and housing

UNHCR general contact

UNHCR general contact provides or coordinates shelter and housing support in or for Juba. Contact the service directly to confirm eligibility, location and current availability. Help depends on status, vulnerability and current operations; accommodation is not guaranteed.

Juba
