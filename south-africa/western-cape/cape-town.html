CAPE TOWN - HOMELESS HELP

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the local emergency services listed below. Conflict, weather and local conditions can disrupt services.

Last verified: 2026-11-24

Information, hours, eligibility and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Start with the most relevant service below and explain that you are in Cape Town, whether you have a safe place tonight, and any medical, safety, disability or child-protection needs.

Shelter and housing

City of Cape Town Safe Spaces

City of Cape Town Safe Spaces provides or coordinates shelter and housing support in or for Cape Town. Contact the service directly to confirm eligibility, location and current availability. Voluntary short-term spaces for adults, with assessment, two meals, showers, storage and social work. Children are not admitted and a bed is not guaranteed.

Culemborg 1 and 2; Ebenezer Road, Green Point; Paint City, Robert Sobukwe Road

capetown.gov.za/local-and-communities/community-health-and-safety/street-people/safe-spaces/

Call or use the official website before travelling. Ask specifically what help is available today and whether a referral or documents are required.
