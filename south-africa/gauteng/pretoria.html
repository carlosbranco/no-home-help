PRETORIA - HOMELESS HELP

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the local emergency services listed below. Conflict, weather and local conditions can disrupt services.

Last verified: 2026-11-24

Information, hours, eligibility and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Start with the most relevant service below and explain that you are in Pretoria, whether you have a safe place tonight, and any medical, safety, disability or child-protection needs.
