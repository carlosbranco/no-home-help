PALIKIR - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: 911. Pohnpei Women's Council safe-house hotline: 923-2000. Pohnpei State Hospital: 320-2213 or 320-3805.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

Palikir is a small administrative capital. Most published services are in nearby Kolonia, about 8 kilometres away. Call first and confirm transport before travelling. No general walk-in homeless shelter is publicly listed; the Pohnpei Women's Council safe house is for people facing violence and requires contact or referral.
