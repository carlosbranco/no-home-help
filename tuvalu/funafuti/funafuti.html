FUNAFUTI - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger or medical emergency: 911 or 999. Princess Margaret Hospital is Tuvalu's only hospital.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

No general walk-in homeless shelter is publicly listed in Funafuti. Contact the Social Welfare Department, Gender Affairs Department, police or Tuvalu Red Cross for assessment and referrals. People escaping domestic violence may be able to receive emergency financial and protection support, but availability must be confirmed locally.
