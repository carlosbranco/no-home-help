TARAWA - HOMELESS HELP

Police emergency: 192. Ambulance: 194. Domestic violence: 188. Women and children 24-hour helpline: 189. Social Welfare emergency cases: 1113.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

No general homelessness shelter is publicly listed for Tarawa. For an unsafe home or violence, call the Kiribati Women and Children Support Centre. For other urgent welfare needs, call Social Welfare on 1113; the service may refer or assess help but accommodation is not guaranteed.
