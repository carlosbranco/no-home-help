Homeless and housing-crisis help in Kingston

For immediate danger or a life-threatening medical emergency, call the local emergency number shown below.

Last verified: 2026-11-24

Shelters and services can be full, change hours or require an assessment or referral. Call before travelling whenever possible. No listing guarantees a bed, housing, treatment, benefits or a job.

If one shelter is full, try the other local services and the referral lines below. Ask about day centres, outreach, food, hygiene, health care and the next safe option; do not rely on a single address.
