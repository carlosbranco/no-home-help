MOGADISHU — GARGAARKA DADKA HOY LA’AANTA AH

Haddii khatar degdeg ah jirto, la xiriir adeegyada degdegga ah ee hoos ku qoran. Colaadda, cimilada ama xaaladda deegaanka ayaa joojin karta adeegyada.

Markii ugu dambeysay la hubiyay: 2026-11-24

Macluumaadka, saacadaha, shuruudaha iyo boosasku way is beddeli karaan. Wac ka hor intaadan tegin. Liisku ma dammaanad qaadayo sariir, cunto, lacag, daaweyn ama shaqo.

Ka bilow adeegga ku habboon ee hoose. Sheeg inaad joogto Muqdisho, inaad haysato meel ammaan ah caawa, iyo baahi caafimaad, badbaado, naafo ama ilaalin ilmo.

Gargaarka degdegga ah

Somali Red Crescent Society

Somali Red Crescent Society wuxuu bixiyaa ama isku xiraa gargaarka gargaarka degdegga ah ee Muqdisho. Si toos ah ula xiriir si aad u xaqiijiso shuruudaha, goobta iyo helitaanka hadda.

Mogadishu

ifrc.org/national-societies-directory/somali-red-crescent-society
