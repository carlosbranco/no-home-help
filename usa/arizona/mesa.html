MESA - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: 911. Mesa street outreach: 602-346-3361. Local information: 211. Crisis support: call or text 988.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Availability, eligibility, hours, and capacity can change. Call ahead when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, job, or legal representation.

Call Mesa Street Outreach at 602-346-3361 for help. The city Off the Streets shelter is referral-only and does not accept walk-ins.
