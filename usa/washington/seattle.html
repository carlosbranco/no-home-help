SEATTLE - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: 911. Shelter referrals: 211. Families needing same-day shelter: 206-245-1026. Crisis support: call or text 988.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Availability, eligibility, hours, and capacity can change. Call ahead when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, job, or legal representation.

Call 211 for current shelter options. Families with children can call 206-245-1026 from 9:00 AM; space is not guaranteed.
