Philadelphia homeless and housing-crisis help

Immediate danger, police, fire, or ambulance: 911
Street outreach for a person living outdoors: 215-232-1984, 24 hours
Food, shelter, benefits, and local referrals: 211 or 866-964-7922; text 898211
Mental-health or substance-use crisis: call or text 988, or call 215-685-6440
Domestic-violence crisis: 866-723-3014, confidential and available 24 hours

Last verified: November 12, 2026

Beds, meals, funding, routes, hours, and eligibility can change. Call or check the linked provider page before traveling when possible. No listing guarantees a bed, meal, treatment, payment, benefit, housing, or job. Call 911 for immediate danger or a medical emergency.

Philadelphia has different shelter intake locations for families, single men, single women, veterans, and young adults. Tell staff your household members, age, gender identity, health or mobility needs, pregnancy, safety concerns, service animal or pet, and whether you need transportation. Ask for a backup plan if no bed is available.

Best first step: during weekday daytime hours, visit Appletree Family Center or Roosevelt Darby Center for assessment. After hours, use the intake location matching your household below. For someone living outdoors, call Street Outreach at 215-232-1984. Placement depends on current capacity and assessment.

1. Urgent help, outreach, and severe weather

Emergency services — 911

Phone: 911.

Call for immediate violence, fire, overdose, unconsciousness, severe breathing trouble, heavy bleeding, serious injury, or another life-threatening emergency. Give the exact location or nearest landmark and describe the danger first.

City of Philadelphia — safety and emergencies

Homeless Street Outreach Hotline

Phone: 215-232-1984, 24 hours.

Call for a person living outdoors who may need shelter, transport, medical care, behavioral-health help, or treatment. Give the exact location, clothing description, observed condition, and immediate risks. Outreach cannot force help or guarantee a shelter bed.

Philadelphia Office of Homeless Services

Office of Homeless Services

Questions: 215-686-7175. Prevention information: 215-686-7177, recorded message.

Ask about shelter intake, homelessness prevention, family needs, or how to correct a problem with a city-funded homeless service. Funding and placement are not guaranteed.

City of Philadelphia — services for people experiencing homelessness

211 Southeastern Pennsylvania

Call: 211 or 866-964-7922. Text: 898211.

Ask for a meal, pantry, shelter, benefits, health care, rent help, utility help, or another local service. Give your ZIP code or nearest cross streets and ask what is open today.

211 Southeastern Pennsylvania

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text: 988. Philadelphia crisis line: 215-685-6440.

Use these lines for suicidal thoughts, an emotional crisis, or an urgent mental-health or substance-use concern. Call 911 when there is immediate physical danger.

Healthy Minds Philly — crisis support

Code Blue cold-weather response

Philadelphia may activate Code Blue when precipitation and temperature or dangerous wind chill create a serious cold risk. During an activation, call Street Outreach at 215-232-1984 for someone outdoors.

Rules and locations can change with the weather. Do not wait for an official activation if a person is confused, very drowsy, shivering uncontrollably, or has pale or numb skin; call 911.

2. Shelter and housing access

City shelter intake — what to expect

City intake staff assess safety, household composition, health, disability, veteran status, and possible alternatives to shelter. They may connect people to shelter, medical or behavioral-health care, treatment, child services, case management, or supportive housing. No assessment guarantees placement.

The city says a household should not be denied shelter entrance solely because it lacks identification, a birth certificate, or guardianship document. Bring papers if available, but do not delay an urgent request because they are missing.

9. Identification, documents, and mail

Free Pennsylvania photo ID for people experiencing homelessness

Pennsylvania waives the initial or renewal photo-ID fee for a person experiencing homelessness. A PennDOT Driver License Center uses Form DL-54A and may require identity and residency confirmation from a shelter or service provider.

Rules differ for replacement cards, driver-license reinstatement, or missing underlying documents. Ask Homeless Advocacy Project for help before paying anyone.

15. A practical plan for today

  1. If there is immediate danger or a medical emergency, call 911. For a mental-health or substance-use crisis without immediate physical danger, call or text 988.
  2. For someone living outdoors, call 215-232-1984. For a shelter request, use daytime city intake or the after-hours location matching the household.
  3. Ask intake for a backup plan, safe waiting place, and transportation if there is no bed. During severe cold, ask whether Code Blue is active.
  4. For food, showers, laundry, mail, and health care today, check Hub of Hope, Broad Street Love, The Center Philadelphia, or Prevention Point. Arrive early.
  5. Use BenePhilly for benefits, PA CareerLink for work, Homeless Advocacy Project for identification or civil legal help, and the tenant hotline for eviction.
  6. Write down each program, the person you spoke with, time, next step, and documents requested. Photograph papers when possible and keep every court or benefit deadline.
