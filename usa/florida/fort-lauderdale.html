FORT LAUDERDALE - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: 911. Broward Homeless Helpline: 954-563-4357. Crisis support: call or text 988.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Availability, eligibility, hours, and capacity can change. Call ahead when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, job, or legal representation.

Call the Broward Homeless Helpline at 954-563-4357 for shelter assessment and referral. Most shelters do not accept direct walk-ins.
