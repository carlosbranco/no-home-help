Miami — help for people without housing

Immediate danger, fire or medical emergency: 911
Homeless Helpline: 1-877-994-HELP (4357) or 305-375-CARE (2273)
Mental health or suicide crisis: 988; community services: 211
County information: 311 or 305-468-5900

Last checked: 11 November 2026

Beds, hours, eligibility and supplies can change. Call before travelling when possible. No listing guarantees a bed, meal, appointment, payment, treatment or job on the same day.

If you do not have a safe place tonight, start with the Miami-Dade Homeless Helpline. Shelter intake is coordinated: do not assume that arriving at a shelter will get you a bed. Say where you are, whether children or violence are involved, and whether you have a health condition, disability, pregnancy, pet or transportation problem.

1. Emergency and first calls

911 — immediate danger

Call: 911.

Use for serious illness or injury, breathing trouble, overdose, fire, violence or immediate danger. Give your location first; an intersection or landmark is useful if you do not have an address.

Miami-Dade Fire Rescue contact information

988 — mental health and suicide crisis

Call or text: 988, 24 hours.

Free, confidential crisis support in English and Spanish, with interpretation for other languages. If there is an immediate medical danger, call 911.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

211 Miami — community services

Call: 211, 24 hours.

Ask for the nearest open food program, crisis support, family help, health care or other social service. Verify the address and intake time before leaving.

211 Miami

311 — Miami-Dade County services

Call: 311 or 305-468-5900. The phone service supports English, Spanish and Haitian Creole.

Use for County services, library locations, weather information, Animal Services and a request for homeless outreach. An outreach request can use an intersection or general area instead of a street address.

Miami-Dade 311

Extreme heat

Miami-Dade libraries, selected parks and government buildings act as cooling sites. Check the live list because a location may close for renovation or have limited hours.

Go to shade or air conditioning, drink water and call 911 for confusion, fainting, seizures or other signs of heatstroke. Homeless outreach teams may distribute water and cooling supplies during alerts.

Current cooling sites

Cold-weather shelter activation

When the County activates its cold-weather plan, extra shelter is coordinated through the Homeless Trust. Call 1-877-994-4357 for the current instructions.

Do not rely on a previous season's location. In medical danger from cold exposure, call 911.

2. Shelter, outreach and day services

Miami-Dade Homeless Helpline — start here

Call: 1-877-994-HELP (4357) or 305-375-CARE (2273); Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.

The Helpline assesses people who are homeless or at risk and connects them with outreach or the coordinated shelter system. Shelter placement depends on assessment and an available, suitable bed.

After choosing a language: option 1 is for people at risk of homelessness, option 2 for domestic violence or trafficking, option 3 for veterans, option 4 for Miami Beach, and option 5 for other Miami-Dade locations.

Official Homeless Helpline instructions

City of Miami Homeless Assistance Program

Walk-in: Stephen P. Clark Government Center, 111 NW 1st Street, 1st floor, Miami, FL 33128

Hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.; information: 311 or 305-416-2080.

Outreach, assessment, shelter and housing referrals, transport help and connections to support services. Going in person does not guarantee a bed or payment.

City homeless assistance

Street outreach request

Call the Homeless Helpline during its hours. Outside those hours, call 311 and ask for homeless outreach. Give the person's location, clothing or other identifying details, immediate risks and whether the person agrees to speak with a team.

The County says an address is not required; an intersection or general area can be used. Outreach is not an emergency service, so call 911 for immediate danger.

How to request outreach

Address: 1603 NW 7th Avenue, Miami, FL 33136

Day services include meals, showers, clean clothing, mail, computers, help obtaining Florida identification, an employment specialist and access to health care.

Emergency shelter placement normally requires a Homeless Trust referral. Use the Homeless Helpline rather than travelling there expecting a bed.

Address: 2020 NW 1st Avenue, Miami, FL 33127

Provides shelter, meals, showers, laundry, case management, clinic access, education and job preparation. Call first and use the Homeless Helpline for coordinated shelter intake; a bed is not guaranteed.

Miami Rescue Mission location and services

Lotus House — women, youth and children

Address: 217 NW 15th Street, Miami, FL 33136

Phone: 305-438-0556. Youth and people escaping domestic violence may email needshelter@lotushouse.org.

Lotus House serves women, youth and children with shelter and support. Contact does not reserve a bed; for general shelter access, also call the Homeless Helpline.

Lotus House contact and shelter guidance

Families with minor children

Call the Homeless Helpline and explain the number and ages of all children, custody arrangements, pregnancy, medical needs and where the family can safely wait.

Ask what documents to bring, but do not delay a call because papers are missing. Placement depends on assessment and family-appropriate availability.

Current family access instructions

Unaccompanied youth and young adults, ages 13–24

Call 1-877-994-4357 and ask for the youth access route. The County lists access partners including Camillus House, Lotus House, Citrus Health Network, Educate Tomorrow, Miami Bridge and Pridelines.

Say your age, whether you are alone, whether an adult is hurting or exploiting you, and whether you need a safe place tonight.

Youth access through the Homeless Helpline

If there is no bed available

Ask for the next safe step, a day center, outreach follow-up and the best time to call again. Explain any health condition, disability, pregnancy, violence risk, child, older adult, pet or inability to travel.

Do not pay anyone who claims to sell a shelter bed. Keep a note of the date, time and number called. Use 911 for immediate danger.

MyACCESS Florida — SNAP, cash and Medicaid

Customer service: 850-300-4323.

Use one application for SNAP food assistance, Temporary Cash Assistance, Medicaid and Refugee Assistance. Applying is free; each program has separate eligibility rules. Paper and community-partner applications are available if online access is difficult.

Florida public-assistance application

Phone: 305-545-2224; Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.

County resource center for referrals and applications involving food, utilities, housing stability, older adults, disability and family needs. Programs and funding vary; call first.

Miami-Dade Community Resource Centers

Culmer Community Resource Center

Address: 1600 NW 3rd Avenue, Miami, FL 33136

Phone: 305-438-4161; Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.

County resource-center information

Homeless prevention and rapid rehousing

The City program can provide temporary rental or utility help to some low-income residents who are homeless or at documented risk. Eligibility, income limits, documentation and available funding apply.

Start with the Homeless Helpline or the City walk-in office. A notice, outreach referral or institutional discharge record may be requested; assistance is never guaranteed.

8. Documents, legal help and immigrants

Florida identification card for a person who is homeless

Florida can waive the identification-card fee when a local homeless shelter provides the required letter verifying homelessness. Identity and lawful-presence documents are still required.

Camillus House lists help registering for a Florida ID. Ask staff to check your exact document list before a licensing-office visit.

Florida driver and identification handbook

Replace a Social Security card

Social Security: 1-800-772-1213, Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m.

Replacement is free. Start online if eligible; otherwise arrange an appointment and ask which original documents are accepted. The card is mailed and is not normally issued the same day.

Social Security replacement-card steps

Legal Services of Greater Miami

Address: 4343 W Flagler Street, Suite 100, Miami, FL 33134

Phone: 305-576-0080.

Free civil legal help for eligible low-income residents of Miami-Dade and Monroe counties, including housing and public-benefit problems. Call for screening; representation is not guaranteed.

Legal Services of Greater Miami

Office of New Americans

Address: Accion Community Center, 970 SW 1st Street, Suite 304, Miami, FL 33130

Phone: 305-636-1010.

Immigration legal-service referrals, naturalization support and connections for newly arrived residents. Call for the current free-consultation schedule.

Office of New Americans contact

Avoid immigration-service fraud

Only an authorised lawyer or accredited representative may give immigration legal advice. In the United States, a notary public is not automatically an immigration lawyer.

For suspicious or improper services, use the County Consumer Mediation Center. Do not hand over original identity documents without understanding why they are needed.

Miami-Dade immigration-service complaints

Safe mailing address

Camillus House provides mail service. Before using it on benefit, court or employment forms, ask how to format the address, how often to check, what identification is required and when unclaimed mail is returned.

Update agencies when your mailing arrangement changes; missing a letter can close a benefit case or cause a court deadline to pass.

9. Women, youth, LGBTQ+ people, veterans and other groups

Domestic violence

Florida Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-500-1119, 24 hours. National hotline: 1-800-799-SAFE (7233).

Ask for safety planning and confidential shelter options. Do not go to a confidential address without instructions. If the person causing harm is nearby or danger is immediate, call 911.

Lotus House domestic-violence contacts

Pridelines — LGBTQ+ support

Miami Beach: 1130 Washington Avenue, 1st floor, Miami Beach, FL 33139

Phone: 305-571-9601.

LGBTQ+-affirming youth, adult and senior programs, mental-health support, health navigation and social connection. The current centre serves youth and young adults ages 13–24. Call to identify the right program; it is not an emergency shelter.

Pridelines current programs

The Trevor Project — LGBTQ+ young people in crisis

Call: 1-866-488-7386; text: START to 678678.

Confidential crisis counselling for LGBTQ+ young people. For immediate physical danger or a medical emergency, call 911.

The Trevor Project crisis services

Homeless veterans

National Call Center for Homeless Veterans: 877-424-3838, free and confidential, 24 hours.

Connects veterans who are homeless or at imminent risk with local VA housing, health, treatment, employment and document support. A family member or neighbour may also call.

Miami VA homeless-veteran care

Miami Vet Center

Address: 8280 NW 27th Street, Suite 511, Miami, FL 33122

Phone: 305-718-3712; Monday–Friday, 8:00 a.m.–4:30 p.m. After hours: 877-927-8387.

Confidential counselling and referrals for eligible veterans, service members and families. Many services accept walk-ins; call if you are unsure about documents or eligibility.

Miami Vet Center

Human trafficking or forced labour

National Human Trafficking Hotline: 888-373-7888; text 233733.

For forced work, commercial sexual exploitation, threats, withheld documents or control by a trafficker. The Homeless Helpline also routes domestic-violence and trafficking callers through option 2. Do not confront the suspected trafficker yourself.

National Human Trafficking Hotline

People who need medical help during evacuation

Miami-Dade's Emergency & Evacuation Assistance Program is for residents who need specialised transportation, medical monitoring or help in an evacuation shelter. Registration should happen before a storm.

Call 311 or 305-468-5900 and explain mobility, equipment, medication, caregiver and transport needs. Registration does not replace 911 in an emergency.

Emergency & Evacuation Assistance Program

Language access

Ask 311, 211, 988, hospitals and benefits offices for an interpreter. Miami-Dade 311 offers English, Spanish and Haitian Creole; other services may use telephone interpretation.

Do not sign a form you cannot understand. Ask for a copy and, for legal or immigration matters, use a qualified interpreter rather than an unknown intermediary.

10. Help with pets

Tell shelter access staff about the pet

Tell the Homeless Helpline about every animal before accepting a referral. Ask whether the animal can stay with you and what safe alternative exists if not. Rules differ by site and no listing here guarantees pet accommodation.

Do not abandon or surrender an animal based only on a verbal promise of a bed.

Miami-Dade Homeless Trust

Humane Society Community Pet Pantry

Soffer and Fine Adoption Center: 16101 W Dixie Highway, North Miami Beach, FL 33160

Phone: 305-696-0800; pantry listed daily, 9:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m.

May provide up to two weeks of pet food or supplies such as carriers, crates and leashes. Items depend on donations, so call before travelling.

Community Pet Support and Pantry

Miami-Dade Animal Services Wellness Clinic

Address: 3599 NW 79th Avenue, Doral, FL 33122

Phone: 311 or 305-468-5900. Vaccinations are walk-in; sterilisation requires an appointment.

Low-cost vaccines and preventive care are available. People receiving specified public benefits may qualify for lower prices and must bring current proof; identification may be required to confirm pet ownership.

Animal Services vaccination and low-income fees

Financial help for veterinary care

The Humane Society lists limited assistance for qualifying Miami-Dade pet owners, including some free or reduced-cost spay and neuter programs. Proof of financial hardship may be required and funds can run out.

Call before scheduling and ask what service, animal, ZIP code and documents qualify.
