AUSTIN - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: 911. Shelter navigation: 512-522-1097. Local information: 211. Crisis support: call or text 988.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Availability, eligibility, hours, and capacity can change. Call ahead when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, job, or legal representation.

Call Sunrise Homeless Navigation Center at 512-522-1097 for shelter navigation. The Marshalling Yard does not accept walk-ins.
