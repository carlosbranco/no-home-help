San Antonio homeless and housing-crisis help

Immediate danger, fire, police, or ambulance: 911
Shelter and Homelink access: 210-207-1799
Haven for Hope: 210-220-2100
Food, benefits, and local referrals: 211
Mental-health or substance-use crisis: 210-223-7233 or 800-316-9241
Domestic violence: 210-733-8810

Last verified: November 12, 2026

Beds, schedules, routes, costs, and eligibility can change. Call before traveling when possible. No listing guarantees a bed, meal, treatment, benefit, money, or job the same day. Call 911 for immediate danger or a medical emergency.

If you have nowhere safe to sleep, call the City of San Antonio Community Connections Hotline at 210-207-1799. Ask for Homelink and say where you are, who is with you, whether anyone is pregnant or under 18, medical or mobility needs, veteran status, pets, and whether you have identification. An assessment or referral is not a promise of a bed or permanent housing.

1. Urgent help and first contact

Emergency services — 911

Phone: 911.

Call for: immediate violence, serious injury, overdose, trouble breathing, unconsciousness, fire, or another life-threatening emergency. Give the exact location and describe the danger first.

San Antonio Police Department contact guidance

Community Connections Hotline and Homelink

Phone: 210-207-1799; published call hours: Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.

Use it for: homeless-services navigation, shelter options, Homelink housing assessment, outreach referrals, and health or social-service connections.

In-person Homelink: Haven for Hope, 1 Haven for Hope Way, Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–3 p.m. American GI Forum also lists limited assessment hours at 610 North Main Avenue, Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, 8:30–11:30 a.m.

Close to Home — current access points

211 Texas

Phone: 211; choose option 1 for local resources or option 2 for state benefits.

Ask for open shelter, food today, health care, benefits, rent or utility help, or a service near you. Ask the operator to confirm hours, eligibility, documents, and space before traveling.

211 Texas

Center for Health Care Services crisis line

Phone: 210-223-7233 or 800-316-9241, 24 hours.

For a mental-health or substance-use crisis, assessment, and direction to the right care in Bexar County. Call 911 for immediate physical danger or a medical emergency.

Center for Health Care Services

San Antonio 311

Phone: 311 or 210-207-6000.

For city services, cooling or warming locations, outreach questions, and non-emergency local information.

San Antonio 311

Street outreach

Request through: Community Connections at 210-207-1799 or 311.

Give a precise location, description, time last seen, mobility or health concerns, and whether there is immediate danger. Outreach is not an emergency response and cannot guarantee transport, shelter, or housing.

2. Shelter and housing access

Haven for Hope — central homeless-services campus

Intake: 1 Haven for Hope Way, San Antonio, TX 78207

Main phone: 210-220-2100; accommodation questions: 210-220-2357.

Published walk-in intake: Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Services can include shelter, meals, showers, health care, documents, benefits, employment, recovery support, case management, and pet kennels.

After hours: a parent or guardian with minor children should speak with Security. A single adult may ask at the South Gate about courtyard pre-intake; this is limited, short-term safe sleeping and does not guarantee admission to a residential program.

North-campus residential eligibility and south-campus courtyard access are different. Bring any ID, medicine, and documents you have, but call if documents are missing. Ask about accessibility, bags, couples, pets, and capacity.

Haven for Hope — get help

Homelink coordinated housing assessment

Phone: 210-207-1799, Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.

In person: Haven for Hope, Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–3 p.m.; American GI Forum, 610 North Main Avenue, Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, 8:30–11:30 a.m.; or another current access point.

The assessment is for possible referral to housing programs. It does not guarantee immediate shelter, a voucher, or permanent housing. Keep your phone number and usual location current.

Close to Home — Homelink

SAMMinistries — emergency shelter and housing programs

Shelter access: call Community Connections at 210-207-1799.

Services: low-barrier emergency shelter, rapid re-housing, permanent supportive housing, veteran programs, and prevention assistance. Each has separate capacity and eligibility.

Rapid re-housing information: 210-377-1616. A call or assessment does not guarantee placement or financial help.

Address: 907 East Quincy Street, San Antonio, TX 78215

Safety planning, emergency shelter, counseling, legal help, and support for adults and children affected by domestic violence. The shelter address is confidential. Call 911 for immediate danger and use a safer device if yours may be monitored.

11. Heat and cold weather

Extreme heat — cooling locations and water

Current locations: call 311 or 210-207-6000. Libraries, senior centers, and community centers may be cooling locations during normal hours.

Heat emergency signs: confusion, fainting, seizure, very hot skin, or inability to drink. Call 911, move to shade or air conditioning, loosen clothing, and cool the person.

Project Cool fans for eligible older adults: call 211. A fan is not safe as the only cooling method in extreme indoor heat.

City heat guidance

Cold weather and severe-weather shelter

Current city information: call 311, 210-207-1799, or 211. Locations and activation thresholds change with the event.

Corazon: may extend hours or provide severe-weather shelter when activated; call 210-904-8001, extension 200.

Emergency signs: confusion, slurred speech, very slow breathing, or inability to wake. Call 911 and move the person somewhere dry and sheltered.
