Houston homeless and housing-crisis help

Immediate danger, fire, police, or ambulance: 911
Shelter, food, benefits, and local referrals: 211 or 877-541-7905
Mental-health crisis: call or text 988; Harris Center 713-970-7000
Domestic violence: 713-528-2121

Last verified: November 11, 2026

Beds, schedules, routes, costs, and eligibility can change. Call before traveling when possible. No listing guarantees a bed, meal, treatment, benefit, money, or job the same day. Call 911 for immediate danger or a medical emergency.

If you are newly homeless, call 211 first. For housing programs, complete a Coordinated Entry assessment through a participating provider such as The Beacon, SEARCH, Healthcare for the Homeless, Star of Hope, or another Coalition partner. An assessment is not a promise of immediate housing. Tell the worker where you are, whether you are safe tonight, medical needs, age, children, pregnancy, disability, veteran status, pets, and whether you have identification.

1. Urgent help and outreach

Emergency services — 911

Phone: 911.

Call for: immediate violence, serious injury, trouble breathing, unconsciousness, overdose, fire, or another life-threatening emergency. Give the exact location and describe the danger first.

City of Houston service guidance

211 Texas / United Way HELPLINE

Phone: 211 or 877-541-7905, free and available 24 hours; text: your ZIP code to 898211.

Use it for: current shelter, meals, food pantries, benefits, health care, domestic-violence help, rent or utility assistance, and other local referrals. Interpretation is available.

Ask the operator to verify: eligibility, intake time, documents, transportation, and whether a bed or appointment is actually available.

211 Texas / United Way HELPLINE

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text: 988, 24 hours.

Use it for: suicidal thoughts, emotional crisis, or concern about someone else. Ask for interpretation if needed. Call 911 when there is immediate physical danger or a medical emergency.

Harris Center crisis guidance

The Harris Center 24/7 Crisis and Access Line

Phone: 713-970-7000 or 866-970-7000.

Service: mental-health crisis support, assessment, service access, and possible Mobile Crisis Outreach Team response for Harris County residents who can voluntarily accept help.

Immediate danger: 911.

The Harris Center — contact and crisis care

Houston 311

Phone: 311 or 713-837-0311.

Use it for: city information, non-emergency service requests, municipal court information, and questions about a city department. For shelter and social-service placement, call 211.

Houston 311

Street outreach

SEARCH Homeless Outreach: 281-994-9613; emergency: 911.

Service: outreach teams engage people living outside and may connect them with Coordinated Entry, health care, identification, benefits, shelter, or housing services.

Important: outreach is not an emergency response and does not guarantee transport or a bed. Give a precise location, description, and urgent health concerns.

Houston outreach contacts

Project Access — free service transportation

Service: scheduled, no-cost transportation between participating homeless-service agencies and health centers. Tickets are distributed through partner sites.

Ask at: The Beacon, Lord of the Streets, Healthcare for the Homeless, or another participating provider for the current map, ticket, and boarding rules.

2. Shelter and housing access

Coordinated Entry — The Way Home

First step: call 211 or visit a participating access point. The Coalition itself does not provide walk-in services.

Service: a standardized assessment for possible referral to housing and supportive-service programs across Harris, Fort Bend, and Montgomery Counties.

Important: completing an assessment does not guarantee immediate shelter, rental assistance, or permanent housing. Keep your contact information current with the provider.

Coordinated Entry — Coalition for the Homeless

5. Health, mental health, and recovery

Medical emergency — 911

Phone: 911.

Call now for: overdose, unconsciousness, chest pain, severe breathing trouble, heavy bleeding, seizure, heat illness, or another life-threatening condition. Do not delay because of insurance or identification.

Healthcare for the Homeless emergency guidance

Healthcare for the Homeless — Caroline Street Clinic

Address: 1934 Caroline Street, Houston, TX 77002

Phone: 713-286-6000.

Published hours: Monday–Thursday, 8 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; Friday, 8 a.m.–noon; no appointments from noon–1 p.m.

Services are free: medical, mental health, behavioral health, addiction counseling, pharmacy, dental, vision, well-woman care, HIV prevention, and care coordination. No insurance, ID, or proof of homelessness is required.

6. Public benefits and income support

Texas benefits help by phone

Phone: 211 or 877-541-7905; choose language, then option 2.

Help with: SNAP food benefits, Medicaid, CHIP, TANF cash assistance, Medicare savings, and case questions. Ask what can be used as proof when you have no fixed address or lost documents.

Texas Health and Human Services — benefits contacts

Your Texas Benefits

Online service: apply for SNAP, Medicaid, TANF, Medicare savings, and other programs; upload documents; check interview dates and application status.

No device or internet: use a library, The Beacon, YARC, or Workforce Solutions computer. Sign out fully and do not save your password on a public device.

Your Texas Benefits

Houston Food Bank Community Assistance Program

Helpline: 832-369-9390.

Navigators can help with: SNAP, TANF, Children's Medicaid, CHIP, women's health coverage, Medicare savings, and related state programs. English, Spanish, and interpreter support are available.

Appointment capacity can close temporarily: call for the current location or walk-in option.

Houston Food Bank benefits assistance

Benefits and income support at The Beacon

Address: 1212 Prairie Street, Houston, TX 77002

Phone: 713-220-9737.

Service: Pathways staff can connect day-center guests with benefits, income supports, Coordinated Entry, mail, Wi-Fi, and partner programs. Arrive early because daily capacity is limited.

The Beacon Pathways services

Lost Lone Star or Medicaid card

Lost or stolen Lone Star Card: 800-777-7328; Medicaid card: 800-252-8263.

8. Identification, legal help, and court

Operation ID — referral-based document help

Organization: Main Street Ministries, 5100 Travis Street, Houston, TX 77002

Phone: 281-833-3500.

Service: helps gather supporting documents, complete forms, and cover costs for Texas ID and birth certificates from any U.S. state, territory, or military birth abroad.

Referral required: no walk-ins or self-referrals. Ask your case manager at a partner agency such as The Beacon, SEARCH, or a shelter to submit a referral.

Operation ID — eligibility and process

Beacon Law — legal help for people experiencing homelessness

Sign-up: The Beacon, 1212 Prairie Street

Published intake: Friday; sign up when doors open at 7 a.m.; first 25 people. A partner case manager can also submit an application.

Cases may include: ID restoration, driver's-license holds, immigration-document replacement, name changes for ID, record clearing, Social Security appeals, and limited eviction help.

Beacon Law

City of Houston birth certificates

Address: Bureau of Vital Statistics, 8000 North Stadium Drive, Houston, TX 77054

Phone: 832-393-4220.

Published walk-in hours: Monday–Thursday, 8 a.m.–4 p.m.; arrive by 3 p.m. Capacity may close the line early. Fees and qualified-applicant rules apply.

If you lack money or supporting ID: ask a case manager for Operation ID or Beacon Law referral before paying an intermediary.

Houston Vital Statistics

Texas state ID or driver's license

Service: Texas Department of Public Safety offices issue identification. Requirements depend on citizenship or lawful presence, identity, Social Security number, and Texas residency.

Before going: use Operation ID, Beacon Law, Lord of the Streets, SEARCH, or YARC to determine which documents and address letter are accepted. Operation ID funding does not replace the required DPS visit.

Texas DPS — apply for an ID card

10. Help with animals

BARC Low-Cost Wellness Center

Address: 3200 Carr Street, Houston, TX 77026

Phone: 832-395-9084; appointments are required.

Services: low-cost microchips, core vaccines, heartworm testing, flea and tick prevention, and wellness packages. It is not a full-service clinic and cannot treat an owned sick or injured pet.

BARC Wellness Center

BARC surrender and low-cost referrals

Service: pet surrender is appointment-based. BARC also lists low-cost spay and neuter partners; free assistance may be available through partner campaigns.

Before surrendering: ask 211, the shelter, and BARC about temporary foster, pet food, veterinary assistance, or a pet-safe placement. Capacity changes daily.

BARC frequently asked questions

Pet and shelter intake

Tell the worker before accepting placement: species, size, temperament, vaccination status, carrier or leash, and medical needs.

Important: the Houston shelter directory does not guarantee that general shelters accept animals. Ask about a pet-friendly option or temporary care before traveling; do not abandon an animal to enter shelter.
