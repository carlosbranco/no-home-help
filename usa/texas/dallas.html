DALLAS - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger or life-threatening emergency: 911. Shelter and homelessness resources: 888-411-6802. City non-emergency help: 311.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Availability and eligibility can change. Call ahead when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, job, or legal representation.

If you need a safe place tonight, call the Housing Forward Crisis Helpline or contact The Bridge or Austin Street Center. Bed availability is not guaranteed.

Emergency services — 911

Call 911 for immediate danger, fire, a life-threatening medical emergency, or a violent situation.

911

Housing Forward Crisis Helpline

Connects people experiencing homelessness with shelter, street outreach, housing support, and community resources. It is not an emergency line and does not guarantee a bed.

+18884116802

dallascityhall.com/departments/housing-and-homelessness/Pages/get-support.aspx

24-hour coverage is indicated by city shelter guidance

Call and explain who needs help, current location, household type, and whether there are urgent health or safety needs.

Emergency shelters and housing

The Bridge Homeless Recovery Center

Free day and night shelter for adults age 18 or older experiencing homelessness. No government ID, background check, or sobriety check is required for intake. Space is not guaranteed. Guest intake: 214-670-1507.

1818 Corsicana Street, Dallas, TX 75201

+12146701100

bridgehrc.org

Night shelter 5:00 PM–6:00 AM; day services 6:00 AM–5:00 PM

Go to intake or call 214-670-1507. Ask about current bed availability.

Your Texas Benefits and 2-1-1 Texas

Apply for or manage SNAP food benefits, Medicaid, TANF, and other Texas benefits. Calling 2-1-1 is free; choose a language and Option 2 for benefits help. Eligibility is not guaranteed.

Other useful resources

Dallas 311

City non-emergency service line. It can connect people to homelessness outreach and city services. Outside Dallas dialing area, call 214-670-3111. A request does not guarantee immediate outreach or shelter.

311

dallascityhall.com/services/311/Pages/about_us.aspx

24 hours for urgent city requests

For shelter and housing resources, also call the Housing Forward Crisis Helpline at 888-411-6802.
