Emergency help

Hennepin Shelter Hotline

Call if you need shelter tonight in Minneapolis or are sleeping outside or in a vehicle. The navigator checks current options; a call does not guarantee a bed.

+16122048200

minneapolismn.gov/government/programs-initiatives/city-federal-response/virtual-resource-center/

24 hours

Tell staff whether you are a single adult, family, young person or need an accessible placement.

Shelter and housing

Higher Ground Minneapolis

Adult emergency shelter with a hot dinner, housing case management and access to homeless health care. Intake is coordinated through the Hennepin Shelter Hotline.

165 Glenwood Avenue, Minneapolis, MN 55405

+16122048200
