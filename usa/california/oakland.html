OAKLAND - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: 911. Shelter and service referrals: 211. Crisis support: call or text 988.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Availability, eligibility, hours, and capacity can change. Call ahead when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, job, or legal representation.

Call 211 for current shelter referrals and outreach. Shelters may require referral and may be full.
