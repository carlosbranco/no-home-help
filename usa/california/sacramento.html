SACRAMENTO - HOMELESS HELP

Immediate danger: 911. Local information and referrals: 211. Mental health or substance-use crisis: call or text 988.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Availability, eligibility, hours, and capacity can change. Call ahead when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, job, or legal representation.

Call 211 and ask for current Sacramento shelter navigation. City-funded shelters usually depend on referral and available capacity.
