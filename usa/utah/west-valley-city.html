Homeless and housing-crisis help in West Valley City

Immediate danger, serious injury, overdose, fire or a crime in progress: call 911. Mental-health or substance-use crisis: call or text 988. Shelter and current bed information: call 801-990-9999.

Last verified: 2026-11-25

Shelter capacity, winter overflow and service hours can change quickly. Call before travelling whenever possible. No listing guarantees a bed, room, housing, appointment, benefit, payment, treatment or job.

Start with the local Switchpoint shelter and the county Homeless Services Line. Say who needs help, whether a partner, children or pet are with you, and any urgent health, safety or accessibility needs. If the local shelter is full or not suitable, ask for another Salt Lake County resource center and the safest transport route.

Emergency help and referral

911 — emergency services

Call for immediate danger, serious injury, suspected overdose, fire or a crime in progress. Give the exact address or nearest intersection and describe the immediate risk.

911

wvc-ut.gov/services/public_safety/index.php

24 hours

Call 911. Do not use a shelter or crisis-line number instead when someone needs an ambulance, fire response or immediate police help.
