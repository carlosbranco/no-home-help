Homeless and housing-crisis help in Salt Lake City

Immediate danger, serious injury, overdose, fire or a crime in progress: call 911. Mental-health or substance-use crisis: call or text 988. Shelter and current bed information: call 801-990-9999.

Last verified: 2026-11-25

Shelters and services can be full, change hours or require an assessment. Call before travelling whenever possible. No listing guarantees a bed, housing, appointment, benefit, payment, treatment or job.

If one shelter is full, call the Homeless Services Line and try another appropriate route below. Say your age, gender or household type, whether children or a pet are with you, and any urgent health, safety, mobility or language needs. Ask about overflow, outreach and the next safe option instead of relying on one address.

Emergency help and referral

911 — emergency services

Call for immediate danger, serious injury, suspected overdose, fire or a crime in progress. Give the exact address or nearest intersection and describe the immediate risk.

911

slc.gov/911/

24 hours

Call 911. Do not use a shelter or crisis-line number instead when someone needs an ambulance, fire response or immediate police help.
