Homeless and housing-crisis help in Provo

Immediate danger, serious injury, overdose, fire or a crime in progress: call 911. Mental-health or substance-use crisis: call or text 988. Community referrals: call 211.

Last verified: 2026-11-25

Utah County does not have a general year-round walk-in overnight shelter. Motel vouchers, warming-center services and housing programs are limited and eligibility-based. Call before travelling. No listing guarantees a room, bed, housing, appointment, benefit, payment, treatment or job.

For a place to sleep, start with Community Action during business hours; after hours use the Provo dispatch route shown below. Food & Care Coalition is the main local day-service hub but is not a general walk-in overnight shelter. If one option cannot help, ask for the next safe local or regional alternative.

Emergency help and referral

911 — emergency services

Call for immediate danger, serious injury, suspected overdose, fire or a crime in progress. Give the exact address or nearest intersection and describe the immediate risk.

911
