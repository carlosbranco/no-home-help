Leeds homeless and housing-crisis help

Immediate danger, serious illness, police, fire or ambulance: 999 or 112
Nowhere safe tonight or homeless: Leeds Housing Options 0113 222 4412; outside office hours 0113 378 8366
Sleeping outside or concerned about someone who is: Leeds Street Outreach 0113 245 9445
Urgent but not life-threatening medical or mental-health help: NHS 111; select option 2 for urgent mental-health support

Last verified: 11 November 2026

Beds, meals, opening times and eligibility can change. Call before travelling when possible. An assessment, application, referral or outreach contact does not guarantee accommodation or another service. Never pay anyone who claims they can secure a council or charity place.

Tell the worker where you are tonight, where you usually sleep, your age, whether children are with you, and whether pregnancy, disability, illness, domestic abuse, immigration restrictions or a pet affects what is safe. If a service cannot help, ask for the next safe option and write down the advice.

Start here: if you have nowhere safe tonight, call Housing Options and say clearly that you are homeless today. If you may lose your home within 56 days, ask for prevention help now. The published assessment wait can be up to four weeks for non-emergencies, so explain any same-day risk clearly.

1. Urgent housing and street outreach

Emergency services

Phone: 999 or 112.

Call for immediate violence, serious injury, overdose, breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness, fire or another life-threatening emergency. Give the exact location or nearest landmark first. If you cannot speak on a mobile call to police, listen and press 55 when prompted.

NHS guidance on calling 999

Leeds Housing Options

Address: Merrion House, 110 Merrion Centre, Leeds LS2 8BB

Phone: 0113 222 4412. Outside office hours call 0113 378 8366. Email: housing.options@leeds.gov.uk.

Open Monday 9am–4pm, Tuesday 10am–4pm and Wednesday–Friday 9am–4pm. Call or attend if you have nowhere tonight. Email if homelessness is likely within 56 days; replies are normally within three working days. Ask to make a homeless application and explain all risks. Emergency or temporary accommodation depends on an assessment.

Housing Options contact and access

Leeds Street Outreach Service

Office: 3rd Floor, Duncan House, 14 Duncan Street, Leeds LS1 6DL

Phone: 0113 245 9445; email: leeds.sos@cgl.org.uk. Office hours are Monday–Friday 8:30am–4:30pm, with outreach on weekdays and evenings.

Give an exact public sleeping location, usual time and description. The team can assess someone sleeping outside and connect them with emergency accommodation and other services. Contact does not guarantee that the person will be found or housed that day.

Leeds Street Outreach

StreetLink

Send an alert about an adult sleeping outside when the location and time are known. StreetLink passes the information to the Leeds outreach team; it is not an emergency service or a bed-booking service. Call 999 for urgent medical danger or a child under 18 who is at risk.

Send a StreetLink alert | council rough-sleeping guidance

Simon on the Streets

Phone: 0113 345 2270; email: admin@simononthestreets.co.uk.

Independent outreach and emotional and practical support for people who may not be engaging with other services. It does not provide emergency accommodation. Use Housing Options or Street Outreach for a same-day housing need.

Support in Leeds

Unsafe home or domestic abuse

Call 999 in immediate danger. For emergency housing call Housing Options on 0113 222 4412, or 0113 378 8366 outside hours. Do not return for papers or belongings if it is unsafe.

Leeds Domestic Violence Service: 0113 246 0401, 24 hours. Accommodation is subject to assessment and availability.

5. Health and dental care

NHS urgent care

Emergency: 999. Urgent but not life-threatening: 111 or NHS 111 online. Say if you have no fixed address, phone, transport or safe place to recover.

NHS 111 online

York Street Health Practice

Address: 68 York Street, Leeds LS9 8AA

Phone: 0113 295 4840. Specialist primary care for people who are homeless or unstably housed, refugees and asylum seekers, and people using substance services.

Current NHS hours are Monday 8am–6pm, Tuesday 8am–1pm and 3pm–6pm, and Wednesday–Friday 8am–6pm; the front door opens at 8:30am. Call first, especially around Tuesday's midday closure.

NHS service details

Register with a GP without documents

GP care is free. You do not need proof of address, immigration status, identification or an NHS number to register. If the form asks for an address, use a temporary address or ask whether the surgery address can be used.

If refused for lack of papers, ask for the refusal in writing and contact NHS England or Citizens Advice.

NHS GP registration rules

Urgent dental care

Call NHS 111 for urgent dental pain, swelling, bleeding or trauma. For routine care, use the NHS dentist search and contact practices directly. Availability and NHS acceptance change frequently.

8. Benefits and emergency money

Leeds Crisis Payment Scheme

The council scheme may help with food or fuel, essential appliances, furniture or carpets, and some removal costs when fleeing violence. Apply online. If you cannot use the form, call 0800 953 2303 or 0113 376 0330, weekdays 9am–4pm, Wednesday from 10am.

A decision is normally aimed for within four working days. Evidence, a bank statement and National Insurance number may be requested. Help is assessed and is not guaranteed immediate cash.

Crisis Payment Scheme

Universal Credit without a fixed address

You can claim while rough sleeping, sofa surfing or staying in a hostel. With permission, use a trusted person's, hostel, day-centre or Jobcentre address. If you lack identification, a bank account, phone, email or National Insurance number, say so: alternatives may be possible.

Tell your work coach about homelessness, mental ill health or recovery needs and ask whether the claimant commitment should change temporarily. In some supported or temporary accommodation, Housing Benefit may cover rent separately.

Universal Credit guidance for homeless people

Phone: 0113 376 0452, Monday–Thursday 9am–4:30pm, Wednesday from 10:15am, and Friday 9am–4pm. Email: welfare.rights@leeds.gov.uk.

Free benefit checks, claims, form filling, challenges and appeals. Deaf or hard-of-hearing people can text 07860 034964 to request an appointment.

10. Documents, phone, mail and storage

Safe mail at St Anne's

St Anne's Resource Centre may provide a safe correspondence address for adults who are homeless or vulnerably housed. Ask first about permission, the exact format, collection times and how long mail is kept.

Do not send post or leave identification, papers or belongings at any service without explicit permission.

St Anne's Resource Centre

National Insurance number

Check old payslips, benefit letters, a tax account or UKVI account first. You may start work before receiving a number if you can prove the right to work, and do not need one to start a benefit claim.

Apply for or find a number

Prove the right to work

British and Irish citizens may use an eligible passport or a birth or adoption certificate together with an official document showing the National Insurance number. Other people may use a share code or eligible immigration documents.

Do not pay an unofficial agent. Ask Citizens Advice or a regulated adviser if status is unclear.

Official right-to-work service

Replace a birth certificate or get passport advice

Order certificates through the General Register Office or relevant register office; fees apply. Contact HM Passport Office before paying if documents, referees or delivery are difficult. A charity may help with an application but cannot guarantee the fee.

Order a certificate | Passport Adviceline

Internet, printing and storage

Leeds Central Library and community hubs provide free internet access; printing may cost. No verified open-access public luggage-storage service was found. Ask a housing or day-centre worker about safe options before leaving anything.

14. Help with pets

Street Paws

Street Paws works in Leeds and Yorkshire, offering veterinary support through referral and helping services consider pet-friendly options. Ask a housing or support worker about referral. Street Paws cannot directly arrange accommodation, fostering or kennels.

Email: info@streetpaws.org.uk.

Street Paws help

StreetVet

People experiencing homelessness can use the UK help form to ask about volunteer veterinary support. Eligibility and local coverage are assessed. StreetVet is not an emergency service and does not cover existing bills or provide fostering.

Request StreetVet help

Dogs Trust Together Through Homelessness

The UK-wide scheme helps eligible dog owners with veterinary costs and finding dog-friendly services. A participating homelessness service may need to apply. Assessment applies; existing bills and every procedure are not automatically covered.

Together Through Homelessness

Tell every service about your pet

Tell Housing Options and every referrer the species, size, health, behaviour and vaccination status. Simon on the Streets reports that one Leeds hostel has two kennels, but access and availability require referral and cannot be promised.

Do not abandon an animal or arrive at an unknown hostel expecting admission.
