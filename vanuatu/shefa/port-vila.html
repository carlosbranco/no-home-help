PORT VILA - HOMELESS HELP

Police emergency: 111 on Digicel or 1111 on Vodafone. Hospital emergency: 112. Violence crisis and safe-house line: 161.

Last verified: 2026-11-23

Information and availability can change. Call before travelling when possible. A listing does not guarantee a bed, meal, appointment, benefit, treatment or job.

No general walk-in homeless shelter is publicly listed for Port Vila. Women and children escaping violence can call the Vanuatu Women's Centre on 161 for confidential crisis support and safe-house referral. For other urgent needs, contact the Ministry of Justice and Community Services or Vanuatu Red Cross and explain the situation.
